Ask us a question
Does my loved one need to learn an app?+

A family member can follow the prompts and handle the recording. Human interview help and optional AI assistance provide another way to begin when the system activates.

Are these real recordings, or AI recreations?+

Real recordings of a living person telling their own story. We never clone, synthesize, imitate, or reconstruct a voice or likeness. No avatars.

Who owns the recordings?+

Your family owns the delivered recordings. You can download your archive and choose which relatives can view or download it. Hosting is a service, not a promise of perpetual storage; keep your own backup.

Can we skip a topic or withdraw a story?+

Yes. The storyteller can skip any question, pause, or stop. A keeper or storyteller can request removal; archive access is hidden immediately while deletion is completed. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Can I give a session as a gift?+

Yes. Tell us which package you have in mind in the contact form. We will help arrange the gift. The storyteller still makes their own decision about participating.

Is this for every family?+

Yes. All races, creeds, and ethnicities are welcome. Our heritage prompts include African American history and can be adapted to your own family’s traditions.

What happens after I request a session?+

Your request is saved for review. A person confirms availability and the next steps. A request is not a confirmed booking or a payment.
