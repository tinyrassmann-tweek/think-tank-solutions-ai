Prioritized next steps for a client

  1. Confirm the real audience, location/service scope and intended conversion.
  2. Verify search access and actual indexing from the owner’s Search Console where permission is available.
  3. Review the agreed pages, factual service descriptions, internal links and crawl behavior.
  4. Separate observed evidence from assumptions; prioritize fixes by impact and effort.
  5. Define the implementation scope and recheck the changed paths after publication.
